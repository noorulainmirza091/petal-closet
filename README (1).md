# Petal Closet

A pastel, whimsical wardrobe companion. Add photos of your clothes and it catalogues each piece, then suggests what to wear today and what to skip.

- Scan: drop in photos and pieces are named, colour-matched and added automatically
- Today: outfit picks by occasion and weather, with reasons
- Maybe not today: pieces to skip, with reasons
- My closet: filter, edit, add and delete pieces

Single-file app: open `index.html`. Photo recognition needs the Claude artifact runtime; elsewhere it falls back to colour matching.
